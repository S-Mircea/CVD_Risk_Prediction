How it works

From your answers to a 10-year estimate, in four steps.

Heartscape combines what you tell us about your health with measured environmental data for your borough, then asks a trained machine-learning model how similar profiles turned out.

01

Tell us about you

Fifteen quick questions: age and sex, blood pressure, cholesterol and BMI, medical history, and a typical week of activity, stress, sleep and alcohol.

02

Add where you live

Your borough brings in six environmental readings: PM2.5, NO₂, noise, green space, walkability and urban heat.

03

The model scores it

All 21 features are scaled and passed to a Random Forest of 200 decision trees. The share of trees voting "at risk" becomes your probability.

04

You get a clear result

A percentage and risk band, the factors pushing it up, your borough's air-quality profile and practical next steps. With a local Llama model running, you also get written advice.
