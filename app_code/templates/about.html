About Heartscape

Heart health doesn't stop at your front door.

Heartscape is a research and education project exploring how the place you live, not just your body, shapes your cardiovascular risk.

Why we built it

Established risk calculators such as Framingham and QRISK are built on clinical and lifestyle markers. They leave out the environment people live in every day, even though air pollution, noise and access to green space are linked to heart disease.

London makes this easy to see: NO₂ levels in some central boroughs are more than double those in leafy outer boroughs. Heartscape adds these borough-level readings to the usual health questions, so your estimate reflects your surroundings as well as your vitals.

How the score is calculated

  • Base risk: the Framingham general cardiovascular risk equation (D'Agostino et al., Circulation, 2008), built from 8,491 participants of the Framingham Heart Study.
  • Environmental adjustment: a multiplier from ×0.85 to ×1.20 for each of the 33 London boroughs, based on average PM2.5, NO₂, noise, green space, walkability and urban heat.

Earlier versions of Heartscape used a machine-learning model trained on 1,000 synthetic records. Testing showed it labelled almost everyone over 40 as very high risk, so we replaced it with the validated equation. See How it works for the details.

What it can't tell you

Heartscape is not a diagnostic tool. Keep these limits in mind:

  • Framingham was developed in a US population and can overestimate risk for some UK groups. GPs in England use QRISK3.
  • It's validated for ages 30–74 and for people without existing heart disease.
  • Family history, weight and lifestyle shape your guidance but aren't in the equation, so your true risk may differ.
  • The borough adjustment is illustrative: pollution affects heart health, but the exact size of the effect here is our estimate.

Your privacy

Your answers are sent to the Heartscape server for a single calculation and are not saved to any database. Written advice, when enabled, comes from a Llama model running locally through Ollama, not from a third-party service.

In an emergency

If you have chest pain, shortness of breath or other signs of a heart attack or stroke, call 999 now. For non-urgent advice, contact your GP or NHS 111.
